Backup privacy notice

29 October 2026

This notice describes the dedicated ISP Planter App backup connection.

Purpose and information

The owner authorizes this application to store and maintain ISP Firebase backup copies in an owner-private Drive folder. Copies can include member and staff records, content, consultation and event-interest records, stored files, account-recovery export data and deployment configuration. Plaintext passwords, authenticator secret keys and live deployment credentials are excluded from backup archives.

Google access

The application requests the limited Google Drive file permission (drive.file). It can manage files it creates and files the owner explicitly selects for it. It does not request access to unrelated Drive files. The account email is checked to ensure the designated owner authorized the connection.

Storage and access

Live app data remains in Firebase. Backup copies are stored in Google Drive. Google provides encryption in transit and at rest. Authorization credentials are kept privately and are intended for Google Secret Manager in the deployed backup process. The application does not create public sharing permissions for backup files, sell Google user data or use it for advertising or AI training.

Owner control

The owner can revoke this application's access in their Google Account connections settings, stopping future backup access. Existing copies remain in the owner's Drive until the owner removes them or an approved retention job removes expired copies. A nightly schedule and 30 daily backup manifests are planned; they are not active until configuration and restoration verification are complete.

For questions, use the support email shown on this application's Google consent screen. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
