Terms of use for the private backup connection

29 October 2026

This connection is configured for the designated owner of the ISP Planter App Firebase project. It is an administrative backup utility, not a public backup service.

  • Authorize only the Google account designated to own the private ISP backup folder.
  • Keep that account and its recovery methods protected. Do not make backup files or authorization credentials public.
  • Only back up information that the app owner is entitled to process and store in the selected Google account.
  • Check backup results and restore evidence before relying on a backup. Granting access alone does not create a working schedule or prove a complete restoration.
  • The owner controls app hosting charges, Drive capacity, retention and who may access any backup copies.
  • Revoke the connection from Google Account settings when it is no longer required. Revocation does not automatically delete existing copies.

Current setup status and remaining limitations are recorded in the project's milestone documentation. Questions can be sent to the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
