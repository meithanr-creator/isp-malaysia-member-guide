ISP Planter App Backups

A private administrative connection that copies the owner's ISP Firebase backup data into a private folder in their Google Drive.

Google permission is limited to files created by, or deliberately selected for, this backup application. It does not request access to the rest of the owner's Drive. This connection is for the designated administrator; members do not need Google Drive permission.

The backup system is being configured. Authorization alone does not mean scheduled backups or restoration have been verified.
